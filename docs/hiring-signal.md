# Hiring Signal

This repository is meant to support hiring conversations for roles that need
delivery ownership and CI/CD literacy.

## What It Proves

- Ability to connect pipelines to feedback, artifacts, and release confidence.
- Awareness of credentials, approvals, rollback, and troubleshooting.
- Communication of delivery risk in practical engineering terms.

## Role Fit

Best aligned with backend, DevOps-adjacent, platform, and release engineering
roles where reliable delivery matters.
